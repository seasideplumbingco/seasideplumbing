About

Honest plumbing you can rely on.

Seaside Plumbing & Mechanical is a husband-and-wife owned company rooted in Southern California and built on quality workmanship, clear communication, and dependable service. Our approach is simple — do the job the right way. No shortcuts. No pressure. No unnecessary upsells.

We know plumbing problems can disrupt your home, business, or project, so we make reliability and communication a priority from the start. We show up when we say we will, explain what we're seeing in plain language, and provide straightforward recommendations so you know what to expect before the work begins.

From residential repairs and maintenance to commercial plumbing and new construction, we take pride in clean, code-compliant work and attention to detail. We work with homeowners, contractors, property managers, business owners, and facility teams to provide plumbing solutions built to last.

When you call Seaside Plumbing & Mechanical, you're working directly with a local, family-owned team that takes pride in its work and its reputation. We're proud to serve San Diego County and surrounding Southern California communities with dependable plumbing and workmanship we stand behind.
